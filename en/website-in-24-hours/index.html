FAQ

Questions about the 24-hour website.

Is it really live in 24 hours?

Yes: within 24 hours of confirming the order the site is published. If we register a new domain for you, the site is already visible on a temporary address and moves to your domain as soon as it is active.

What if you’re late?

If the site isn’t live within 24 hours of confirmation, you don’t pay. It’s in the contract.

Does it work at weekends?

Yes: 24 calendar hours. Confirm on Saturday morning, live on Sunday morning.

Any costs from the second year?

Domain and hosting are included for the first year. The renewal depends on the domain you choose; we agree it and put it in the contract before starting, so you know the cost upfront.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing. A 15-minute call or WhatsApp chat about your business is enough. We handle copy, images and structure.

How much does it cost?

€350 including VAT, one-off, for a brochure site of up to 5 pages. Google and Bing SEO is optional at €150. Alternatively €24.80/month with domain, hosting and maintenance included (24-month minimum).

What if I don’t like it?

You don’t pay. We show you the website before payment; if it doesn’t convince you, you stop there at no cost.

Do you work outside Italy?

Yes: the 24-hour website is done entirely remotely, in English, Italian, German, French or Spanish.

Can it grow later?

Of course. Add pages, a blog, SEO or turn it into a larger site without starting over.
