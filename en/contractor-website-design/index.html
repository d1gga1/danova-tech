The reality

Local search
is the whole game

Homeowners search for a service plus a place — "AC repair Mesa", "roof replacement Plano" — or just "near me". The map results and the first few organic results take almost all the calls.

Winning there takes three things: a complete Google Business Profile with the right categories and service areas, a steady flow of genuine reviews, and a site with a real page for each service and area. Lead-gen marketplaces sell you the same lead as three competitors; your own rankings do not.

A note on honesty: Google requires a real address for a business profile, and fake offices get suspended. We build service-area pages for the places you serve, and leave the profile tied to your real location.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Do you work with ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Yes. We connect booking and quote forms to the platform you use where it offers an API or booking integration, so leads land straight in your schedule.

Can you build pages for every city we serve?

Yes, as long as each page has genuinely useful content for that area. Thin copy-paste city pages do not rank anymore, and we will not build them.

How fast will we get calls?

From Google Ads, immediately. From organic search, typically two to six months for area-specific searches, depending on competition and reviews.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
