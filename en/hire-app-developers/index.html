The reality

Freelancer, agency
or team for hire?

A freelancer is great for a small, well-defined job. For an app your business depends on, the risk is continuity: one person, one point of failure.

A big agency covers every role but prices in its overhead. A small team covers the roles without the overhead — that is the gap we fit.

Hourly staff augmentation can work with a strong internal product owner. If you do not have one, a fixed-price-per-stage project with a team that also does analysis is usually safer.

How it works

Fixed price per stage,
not an open meter.

After a free call and a short analysis, you get a fixed price for the first stage — usually the smallest version that is useful — and a working build every week.

You can stop after any stage and keep everything: code in your repository, apps under your developer accounts, IP assigned to your company.

Calls happen in your morning, our afternoon; the build happens while you are offline.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Can I hire developers by the hour?

We prefer fixed-price stages because they protect you from open-ended costs. For ongoing work after launch, we can agree a monthly allocation.

Can your developers join our existing team?

Yes, for well-defined workstreams, with code in your repository and your review process.

Do you sign NDAs and assign IP?

Yes, both, as standard.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
