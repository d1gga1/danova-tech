The reality

Why affordable
does not mean cheap.

Website builders look cheap until you count your own hours, the apps you add and the template everyone else uses. Big agencies are good but price in their office and their account team. A small business is usually best served by something in between: a real developer, a fixed small price, and a site you own.

Our price is low because our overhead is low — a ten-person team in northern Italy — not because we skip the parts that matter: speed, structure, accessibility and search foundations are in every site.

Two options, no tricks: €350 one-off and the site is yours (plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing), or €24.80 a month with everything included on a 24-month minimum term.

Getting found

Local search
is where small businesses win.

You will not outrank national directories on broad terms, and you do not need to. Searches like "bakery in Brookline" or "roof repair Mesa" are decided by your Google Business Profile, your reviews and a site that clearly says what you do and where.

We set up the structure for that from day one: a page per service, your service area stated clearly, structured data for your business, and a simple way to ask happy customers for reviews.

If you later want ongoing SEO or Google Ads, that is a separate service with no long contract.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Is €350 really the whole price?

For a standard small-business site, yes: design, development and launch. Add €150 for the initial Google and Bing optimization. Domain and hosting are yours to keep; on the monthly plan they are included.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, text and images. On the monthly plan we do changes for you as part of the fee.

How fast can it go live?

Usually two to three weeks once we have your texts and photos. We can help write the texts.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
