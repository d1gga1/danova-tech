Exhibition stands · Italy & Europe

Exhibition stand builder in Italy: turnkey stands for your trade fair.

We design, supply and build trade fair stands for companies from every country and industry exhibiting in Italy and across Europe. We source the materials and every component required, and our installation crews build your stand right at the fair. Over 25 years of experience, one contact person from the first sketch to dismantling.

Everything your stand needs, from one supplier

Exhibiting abroad means dealing with unfamiliar rules, suppliers and logistics. We handle all of it on the ground, so you can focus on your customers.

Design and 3D renders

Electrical installation with the certificate of conformity Italian fairs require, lighting, carpet, screens and LED walls.

Why work with a local partner in Italy

Italian exhibition centres have their own technical regulations, deadlines and procedures, and they are strict about them. A local partner who knows them saves you time, money and last-minute surprises.

We work with modular aluminium and stretched-fabric systems, preassembled stands and custom-built wooden stands, all available to rent or to buy. If you exhibit in Italy every year, we can store your stand between shows and have it ready for the next one.

  • Write in your language. Our team replies to every request, in your language or in English.
  • Urgent requests accepted. Even close to the opening day, we check right away what can be done.
  • Rent or buy. Preassembled, modular, fabric and custom stands, rented or owned.
  • Italy and Europe. The same organisation at Italian fairs and at shows in Germany, France, Spain and the rest of Europe.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked.

How much does an exhibition stand in Italy cost?

It depends on floor space, type of structure, finishes, electrics, logistics and the city of the fair. We do not publish a price list: we send you an itemised quote for your project.

Can you build a stand designed by another company?

Yes. We can handle only materials, components and installation, following your designer’s drawings.

Do you accept last-minute requests?

Yes. With our own crews and partner crews we can often organise a stand close to the opening day.

Can the stand be reused at other fairs?

Yes. Modular aluminium and fabric stands are built to be reused, and we can store them between shows.

Do you deal with the fair organiser?

Yes: project approval, documents, passes and loading times.

Which language can I write in?

Your own language or English: we reply to every request.

Exhibiting in Italy or Europe?

Tell us the fair, the dates and your floor space. We will reply with what you need and what it costs.
