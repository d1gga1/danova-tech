Software cost guide

How much does
custom software cost?

Custom software is priced by scope, integrations and risk — not by a list. Here is how to estimate it, how to compare it against a SaaS subscription over three years, and what our own fixed prices look like.

Get a fixed quote Read the guide
What drives the price

The cost drivers,
in order

01

Process complexity

How many roles, steps, exceptions and approvals the software has to model. Messy processes cost more to build — and are exactly where off-the-shelf tools fail.

02

Integrations

Accounting, ERP, CRM, e-commerce, email. Good APIs make integrations predictable; legacy systems without them make them expensive.

03

Data migration

Moving years of spreadsheets or an old system's data, cleaned and checked. Often underestimated.

04

Users and devices

Office only, or field staff on phones, offline, in several languages.

05

Security and compliance

Roles, audit logs, encryption, HIPAA-conscious hosting where health data is involved.

06

Who builds it

Rates differ greatly between onshore agencies, offshore teams and European studios like ours.

Buy vs build

The three-year
math

Compare total cost over three years, not the first invoice. A SaaS tool at a per-seat price, multiplied by users and years, plus the hours your team loses working around the parts that do not fit, is the real number.

If a SaaS product fits ninety percent of your process, buy it — and integrate it. If it fits sixty percent, the missing forty is where the hours go, and custom software built in stages usually pays back within the first year or two.

The middle path is common and often best: keep the platform, build the missing module, interface or integration around it.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How much does custom software cost for a small business?

A focused tool starts from €1,500 with us; most small-business systems land in the low five figures in euros, built in stages.

Is custom software cheaper than SaaS?

Over three years it often is when per-seat fees are high or the SaaS fits poorly. When a SaaS fits well, buying is cheaper.

How long does custom software take?

The first usable stage typically goes live in four to eight weeks.

What are the ongoing costs?

Hosting under your account and optional maintenance. No license fees to us.
