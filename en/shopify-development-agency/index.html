The reality

Most slow stores
are slow because of apps.

The typical Shopify store we audit runs a dozen apps, each adding scripts to every page. Speed drops, conversion drops, and the monthly app bill climbs. Rebuilding the theme with the essential features built in is often the highest-return project a brand can do.

SEO on Shopify comes down to structure: collection pages with real content, product pages with unique descriptions and structured data, clean URLs and no duplicate filtered pages in the index.

Sales tax, shipping rules and accessibility are configured properly from the start, not discovered after launch.

When not Shopify

Sometimes the answer
is custom.

Complex B2B pricing, product configurators with real engineering rules, unusual fulfillment or marketplace models can outgrow Shopify. In those cases we build custom e-commerce and connect it to the same back office.

We will tell you which on the first call, with reasons. Most brands under eight figures in revenue are best served by a well-built Shopify store.

Projects get a fixed quote; you own the store, the theme code and all accounts.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Do you work with Shopify Plus?

Yes, including checkout extensibility, B2B features and Shopify Functions for custom discounts and logic.

Can you speed up our existing store?

Usually, yes. We audit apps and theme code, remove what is not needed and rebuild the essentials natively.

Will our SEO survive a migration to Shopify?

Yes, if it is planned: URL inventory, 301 redirects, content preserved, and checks after launch.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
