Outdoor paving in a few lines

Outdoor paving lasts only if what is underneath is right: a stable base, correct falls to the drains, waterproofing on terraces above living spaces, movement joints and frost-resistant, non-slip materials. FAQ

Questions about outdoor paving.

Do I need a permit to redo outdoor paving?

Replacing existing paving is often free building activity in Italy, but it depends on the municipal rules, the required permeable area and any restrictions. We check before starting and prepare the filing if needed.

How much does it cost to redo a terrace or courtyard?

It depends on area, demolition, base condition, waterproofing and material. We do not publish price lists: after the free survey you receive a written fixed-price quote.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We come to the property, measure, check systems and structures and give you a written, itemised quote. You pay nothing and commit to nothing.

What does fixed price mean?

The price written in the contract is the final price for the work described. If during the job you decide to add or change something, we quote it in writing first: no extra item appears on the invoice without your approval.

How do you guarantee deadlines?

Start date, completion date and intermediate stages are written into the contract, together with the work schedule. Crews and materials are planned before the site opens, so the calendar does not depend on last-minute availability.
