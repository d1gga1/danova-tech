Generative engine optimization: be the source AI assistants quote.

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Mode instead of scrolling through ten blue links. Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work that makes your company understood, trusted and cited in those answers. It builds on SEO, and adds what AI assistants specifically need.

Check your AI visibility What GEO is
In breve

GEO, in five lines

Generative engine optimization (also called AI search optimization or AEO) improves how AI assistants describe and cite your business. It works on five levers: clear, direct answers on your pages, verifiable facts (prices, timings, specs), structured data consistent with visible text, entity signals (who you are, where, since when, what others say about you) and technical access for AI crawlers. We measure results by asking assistants the questions your customers ask, every month.

What we do

Six levers of generative engine optimization

01

Answer-first pages

Each key page opens with a two- or three-line direct answer, followed by details. That is the part AI assistants quote most.

02

Facts, not adjectives

Prices, delivery times, standards and numbers stated clearly. Vague copy is skipped; precise copy is cited.

03

Structured data

Organization, services, offers, FAQs and locations in schema.org, consistent with what users see.

04

Entity consistency

Same name, address, description and services across your site, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles.

05

AI crawler access

robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and others, llms.txt and fast, readable HTML.

06

Monthly testing

We ask the assistants your customers’ questions and track whether and how your company appears, and who appears instead.

Our own site

We test it on ourselves first

This website runs in eight languages with an open robots.txt for AI crawlers, a public llms.txt, speakable and FAQ structured data on hundreds of pages and a direct-answer box at the top of every service page. Every technique we sell is applied here and measured.

GEO does not replace SEO: AI assistants still rely heavily on search indexes, and Google AI Mode cites pages Google has indexed. That is why our GEO work always starts with a technical SEO check, and continues with content and mentions on other sites.

FAQ

Generative engine optimization FAQ

What is generative engine optimization?

GEO is the practice of making a website and a company easy for generative AI engines such as ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity and Copilot to understand, trust and cite in their answers.

Is GEO different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. Indexing, quality content and authority still matter; GEO adds answer-first content, verifiable facts, structured data and entity consistency that AI answers rely on.

How do you measure GEO results?

By asking AI assistants the questions your customers ask, every month, and tracking mentions, citations and links to your site, together with Search Console and analytics data.

Does llms.txt help with ChatGPT?

It can help some assistants and tools understand your site faster, but it is a proposal, not an official standard, and Google does not use it for search. We treat it as a small extra, not the core of GEO.

How long does GEO take?

Technical and structural fixes can be done in weeks; being cited more often depends on content and external mentions, and usually takes a few months.

What do AI assistants say about your industry?

We ask ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity your customers’ questions and tell you who gets cited and why.
