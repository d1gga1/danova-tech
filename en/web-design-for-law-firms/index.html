Law firms

Law firm websites
that bring cases.

A law firm website has two jobs: rank for the specific matters you want, and turn a stressed visitor into a consultation. Most fail at both because they are built as brochures. We build them as intake systems — accessible, fast, and measured on signed clients, not page views.

The reality

Legal search is expensive
to buy, cheaper to earn.

Legal keywords are among the most expensive clicks on Google Ads — personal injury terms in big cities can cost well over a hundred dollars a click. Organic rankings for specific matters are slower to build but cost nothing per click once they hold.

The firms that win organically do it with depth: a real page for each practice area in each place you practice, answering the questions clients ask before they call. Generic "we fight for you" pages do not rank and do not convert.

Your Google Business Profile matters as much as the website for local matters: categories, service areas, photos, and a steady, rules-compliant flow of reviews.

The rules

Accessibility, advertising rules
and confidentiality

Law firm sites are a frequent target of ADA website claims, which is awkward for a firm. Every site we build follows WCAG 2.1 AA and is tested with keyboard and screen readers before launch.

State bar advertising rules differ — on testimonials, case results, specialization claims and disclaimers. We build the structure and leave the wording decisions to you; we will flag anything that commonly needs a disclaimer.

Forms do not create an attorney-client relationship unless you say so, and the site should say that clearly. Portals use encryption, access control and logging, and hosting sits under your firm's account.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

How much does a law firm website cost?

A focused firm site starts from €350 one-off; most firms need more practice-area pages, intake routing and integrations, and get a fixed quote after a free call. Client portals are quoted per stage.

Can you integrate with Clio, MyCase or our practice management software?

Where the software offers an API — Clio and MyCase do — we can send intake leads straight into it, so nobody retypes them.

Do you write the content?

We draft practice-area pages from an interview with your attorneys, who correct and approve them. That is the only way legal content ends up both accurate and readable.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
