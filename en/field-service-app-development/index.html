Field service apps
that technicians actually use.

Field service software fails in the field, not in the demo: too many screens, no signal in the basement, forms built for the office. We build field apps around the technician's day — jobs, checklists, photos, signatures, parts, invoices — working offline and synced with the office.

The reality

Three screens,
not twelve fields.

The main reason field software gets abandoned is not technical: closing a job takes too long. If a technician has to fill twelve fields on a screen designed for a desk, the fields stay empty and the data is useless.

We design for one hand and gloves: open the job, take photos, tick the checklist, sign, done. Offline by default, because basements, plant rooms and rural sites have no signal.

QR codes on equipment take it further: scan and see the asset's full history, manuals and last service.

Buy or build

When custom
makes sense

If a platform like ServiceTitan or Jobber fits how you work, use it — we can integrate with it. Custom makes sense when your process is specific: regulated inspections, complex equipment, multi-step jobs, or per-seat fees that no longer add up.

We build in stages: the first, usually work orders and the mobile app, goes live in four to eight weeks.

Custom software starts from €1,500 for a focused first stage, with a fixed price per stage.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on the device and syncs automatically when the connection returns.

Can it integrate with QuickBooks?

Yes, invoices and customers sync through QuickBooks' API, as with most accounting systems.

Can we use it on Android and iPhone?

Yes, on both, and on tablets.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
