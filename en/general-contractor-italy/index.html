In short

What is a general contractor

A general contractor is the company that takes responsibility for the entire building project towards the client: it signs one contract, coordinates design, permits, specialist trades and supplies and guarantees price and deadlines. The client does not have to manage dozens of suppliers: there is one point of contact. Danova Real Estate acts as general contractor for homeowners, businesses, hotels and property investors in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia, Italy.

The advantage

Why it beats separate contracts

With separate contracts the client signs many agreements and becomes, in effect, the site coordinator: matching timings, interfaces between companies and responsibilities. Every delay by one crew affects the others and it is often unclear who should pay for it.

With a general contractor all these interfaces are our problem. The price is set beforehand, there is one calendar and responsibilities do not bounce around: if something goes wrong you know exactly whom to turn to.

It is the right formula for hotels, shops, offices and restaurants, income properties and complete renovations of high-end homes.

FAQ

Questions about general contracting.

What is the difference between a general contractor and a builder?

A builder carries out the works within its trade; a general contractor takes responsibility for the entire project, including work entrusted to other specialist companies, and answers to the client with a single contract.

Is a general contractor more expensive?

Coordination has a value, but it is usually recovered by avoiding delays, overlaps between trades and unplanned variations. Above all, the price is set in advance: you know what you will spend.

Do you work with my designers?

Yes. We can develop the design ourselves or work with your architect or engineer, handling execution, supplies and coordination.

Do you work for investors who do not live in Italy?

Yes: we manage site, suppliers and permits locally and report with photos and video calls in English or Romanian.

Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We come to the property, measure, check systems and structures and give you a written, itemised quote. You pay nothing and commit to nothing.

What does fixed price mean?

The price written in the contract is the final price for the work described. If during the job you decide to add or change something, we quote it in writing first: no extra item appears on the invoice without your approval.

How do you guarantee deadlines?

Start date, completion date and intermediate stages are written into the contract, together with the work schedule. Crews and materials are planned before the site opens, so the calendar does not depend on last-minute availability.

A project to entrust to a single manager?
