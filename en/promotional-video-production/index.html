FAQ

Questions about promotional videos.

How much does a promotional video cost?

It depends on length, number of versions and technique, so we send a fixed written quote within 24 working hours of your request.

How fast is it ready?

In 24 hours from when we approve the concept and copy together. For series or campaigns with many variations we agree a release schedule.

Do you need to film?

No. We work with AI-generated video, 3D motion design and animated graphics. If you already have photos or clips of the product, we use them.

Which formats will I get?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories, 1:1 and 4:5 for feeds, 16:9 for YouTube, websites and LED walls, with or without captions and voice-over.

Can you make videos in English?

Yes: on-screen text and voice-over in English, Italian, German, French or Spanish. The samples on this page are in Italian.

Who owns the video?

You do: use it in ads, on your website, in stores and at trade fairs. Music and assets are licensed for commercial use.

What video do you need?

Tell us what you sell and where you want to publish: within 24 working hours we send you a concept, format and quote.
