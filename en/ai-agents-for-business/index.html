In breve

AI agents at Danova Tech, in five lines

An AI agent is software that uses a language model (like those behind ChatGPT or Claude) to complete a multi-step task: it reads a request, looks up your data, uses your tools (ERP, CRM, email, WhatsApp) and produces a result, stopping for human approval where the rules say so. Danova Tech builds AI agents for SMEs: we start with one precise process, measure the time saved and only then extend. This is what people mean by agentic AI in a business setting.

Method

How we build an AI agent

  1. Pick one processRepetitive and measurable: how many requests a day, how long each, how many errors. That is the number to beat.
  2. Write the rulesWhat the agent does alone, what it prepares for approval, what it never touches. Separate permissions per system.
  3. Prototype on real dataTested on past real requests, in parallel with business as usual, without touching production data.
  4. Measure and decideTime and errors compared with the baseline. If the result is not there, we tell you and stop.
  5. Go liveEvery action logged, alerts on exceptions, running costs under an agreed cap. Then the next process, if it pays.

FAQ

AI agents FAQ

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent completes a multi-step task in your systems: it reads a request, checks data, drafts an order or quote and asks for confirmation before important actions.

What does an AI agency do?

An AI agency designs and builds AI solutions for companies: assistants, AI agents, automations and integrations with existing software. We also handle the integration with ERP and CRM, which is where most projects succeed or fail.

Will our data be used to train AI?

No. We use services that contractually exclude training on customer data, choose with you where data is processed and, for very sensitive documents, can run a model on your own server.

How long does the first AI agent take?

It depends on the process and on how accessible your data is. You receive a fixed-price quote per phase, with timing, within 24 hours of the first call.

AI agent or n8n automation?

If data always arrives in the same format, a classic automation is cheaper and makes fewer mistakes. An AI agent pays off when requests are free text or change every time. Often the right solution uses both.

Which process eats most of your hours?

Tell us in a 20-minute call: we will say whether an AI agent pays off, whether a simple automation is enough, or whether to leave it.
