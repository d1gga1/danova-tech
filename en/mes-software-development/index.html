MES software

Custom MES software
for the shop floor.

A manufacturing execution system connects the plan in your ERP with what really happens on the floor. Packaged MES suites are powerful and expensive to fit. We build focused MES and scheduling tools for small and mid-size plants — the modules you need, connected to the ERP you have.

The reality

Start with one line,
not the whole plant.

MES projects fail when they try to model the entire plant before anything goes live. We start with one line or one department, prove the data is right and the operators use it, then extend.

Operator screens are designed for gloves and noise: big buttons, few taps, badge login.

We come from northern Italy's manufacturing districts, where we build this kind of software for machinery and component makers.

Integration

Connected to
your ERP and machines

Work orders and items come from your ERP; production results go back. We integrate with Epicor, SAP Business One, NetSuite, Dynamics and others through APIs or database exports.

Machine data can come from standard protocols such as OPC UA or MQTT when available, or from simple operator inputs when not.

A focused first stage starts from €1,500 with a fixed price.

FAQ

The questions we always get.

Do we need an MES or just better ERP use?

If your problem is planning, the ERP may be enough. If it is knowing what happened on the floor and when, you need MES functions. We tell you which after a visit or call.

Can you read data directly from our machines?

Often, if they expose OPC UA, MQTT or similar. Otherwise operators log events in a few taps.

How long does a first stage take?

Typically six to ten weeks for one line or department.

How much does a website cost?

A company website costs €350 one-off, plus €150 if you want the initial optimization for Google and Bing, or €24.80 a month with hosting, domain, maintenance and content changes included on a 24-month minimum term. Web apps start at €50 a month or €750 one-off; custom business software starts at €1,500. Larger projects get a fixed quote after a free analysis.

Will we own the website and the code?

Yes. On the one-off option the domain, hosting, content and source code are registered in your company's name from go-live, and the code lives in your repository. There is no license tied to us and no lock-in.

Do we pay VAT or sales tax on your invoice?

Our invoices to US businesses are issued without Italian VAT, because business services sold outside the EU are not subject to it, and as a foreign service provider we do not add US sales tax. Your accountant will confirm how to record it on your side — it is a standard cross-border arrangement.

Got a project in the US?

Thirty minutes on a video call, free and with no sales deck: we tell you what you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live. If we are not the right fit, we tell you that too.
