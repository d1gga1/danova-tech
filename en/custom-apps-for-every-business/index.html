FAQ

Questions about custom apps.

How much does a custom app cost?

Web apps start at €50/month or €750 one-off; more complex apps with iOS, Android and integrations have a fixed price after a free analysis.

How soon will I see something?

A working demo within 48–72 hours of the first call; the first version in use usually within a few weeks.

App store app or web app?

For internal use and many customer services an installable web app is faster and cheaper. Store apps make sense when advanced notifications, device features or store presence matter.

Do you work with clients outside Italy?

Yes: we work remotely across Europe and the US, in English.

Who maintains it?

We do, under a support contract: OS updates, backups, fixes and new features.
