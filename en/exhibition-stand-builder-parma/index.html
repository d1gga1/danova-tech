Stands in Parma, from design to installation

Where we work: Fiere di Parma.

Fiere di Parma sits in the heart of Italy's Food Valley. It hosts Cibus, the international exhibition of Italian food, Cibus Tec for food processing and packaging technology, SPS Italia for industrial automation and several large public fairs.

Food and machinery shows here mean refrigeration, water and serious electrical loads. We plan them in the project and order them from the organiser in time, so the stand works from the first hour.

What to know about building a stand in Parma

  • Food Valley shows. Tastings, refrigeration and hygiene are at the centre of food stands.
  • Machinery too. Cibus Tec and SPS Italia need bases, power and compressed air for running machines.
  • Collective stands. Consortia and regional pavilions are common at Cibus: we design coordinated stations.
  • Certificates included. Electrical declaration of conformity and fire-rating certificates are part of our service.

Exhibiting from China or from abroad

Most of our international clients have never worked with an Italian stand builder before. This is how we make it simple.

  • One contact person. From the first sketch to dismantling you talk to the same person, by email, phone or WhatsApp: in English, or in Chinese with the help of translation tools.
  • Our own crews, warehouse and production. Installation crews, warehouse and in-house production of all wood-based structures and furniture: fewer subcontractors, more control over quality and deadlines.
  • Large and two-storey stands. From small shell scheme stands to custom stands of over 300 m², including two-storey stands with meeting rooms upstairs.
  • We work with your design. If your designer in China has already drawn the stand, we build it, check it against the Italian technical regulations and propose changes only where needed.
  • Contract with your company. We sign and invoice directly with your company, in China or abroad, with an itemised quote in euro.
  • Approvals and certificates. We submit drawings to the organiser and provide the electrical declaration of conformity and fire-rating certificates.
  • Your freight forwarder, our schedule. Your exhibits travel with your forwarder; we coordinate unloading slots and times so goods and stand arrive in the right order.
  • Storage for the next edition. If you exhibit every year, we keep your stand in our warehouse in Italy and bring it back for the next show.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked.

Do you build stands at Fiere di Parma?

Yes. We design, supply and install stands at Fiere di Parma, and handle approvals and certificates with the organiser.

Can you build at short notice?

We assess urgent requests straight away: with modular structures and fabric graphics we can often deliver a few weeks before opening.

How much does a stand for Parma cost?

It depends on floor space, type of structure, finishes, electrics and services. We do not publish standard prices: we send you a free itemised quote for your project.

Can we communicate in Chinese?

Yes. Write to us in Chinese or English: we reply in Chinese with the help of translation tools and confirm important technical details in English too, to avoid misunderstandings.

Is the stand rented or bought?
