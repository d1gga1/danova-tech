How to choose

Monthly fee or one-off payment?

The monthly plan works if you would rather not put money on the table at the start and prefer domain, hosting, maintenance and changes to sit in one line you never have to think about again. At €24.80 a month the site is live, updated and supported, and small changes do not turn into a quote every time.

Paying once works if you would rather close the cost and carry no commitment. At €350 the site is yours; €150 more gets it properly set up to be found on Google and Bing, and from then on you pay nothing beyond domain and hosting.

One thing we say reluctantly, but we say it: a high, vague monthly fee — from anyone — is often the price of the website spread over instalments. Ours is deliberately low because it is not that: it is the service, not the site hidden inside a payment plan. If after 24 months you want to move to owning the site, we sort it out without drama.

Transparency

What the price includes,
and what it does not

Every figure covers design, development, going live and the checks that make the page fast and readable on a phone. There is no activation fee and no line item that appears once the work is done.

Outside the price sit the things that depend on you: any third-party licences, professional photography if you want a studio to shoot it, advertising budget on Google or Meta (that is your spend, not our fee) and anything you ask for after delivery, which we quote separately and only if you ask.

On larger projects — an ERP, an integration with your invoicing system — the figure comes after a free analysis. It is the only way to give a number that still holds at the end.

FAQ

How much does a website cost?

With us a website costs €24.80 per month including VAT, with domain, hosting, maintenance and content changes included and a 24-month minimum term; or €350 as a one-off if you prefer to pay once and own it. In that second case SEO optimisation for Google and Bing costs a further €150, so €500 in total.

What does the €24.80 monthly fee include?

It includes the domain, hosting with an SSL certificate, maintenance with backups and security updates, text and image changes, and support. There is no setup fee: you start with the first month. The minimum term is 24 months.

What is the €150 SEO for Google and Bing?

It is the initial search engine optimisation of the site: page structure, titles and descriptions, structured data, sitemap, registration on Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submission of the pages for indexing. It is a one-off job and it is added to the €350 website.

How much does a web app cost?

A web app costs €50 per month including VAT, with hosting, updates and support included, or from €750 as a one-off if you choose a ready-made application, which is running from day one.

How much does a business ERP cost?

A custom management system starts at €1,500 including VAT. The exact figure is set after a free analysis of your processes, because it depends on how many modules you need, how much data has to be migrated and which systems it must talk to. Once given, the price is fixed and does not change during the project.

Do the prices include VAT?

Yes. Every figure on this page includes VAT: what you read is what you pay.

Are there hidden or activation fees?

No. We charge no activation, setup or exit fees. The only extras are the ones you ask for after delivery, and you always get a written quote before we do them.

Do I own the website?

With the one-off payment, yes, from the day it goes live: domain, hosting and content are in your name. On the monthly plan we manage the site for the 24-month minimum term, but the content stays yours and at the end you can move to owning it without rebuilding everything.

How do we get to a quote?

With a free half-hour call in which we work out what you need. Then you get a fixed-price quote covering everything required to go live. If it turns out the smaller option is enough for you, we say so.

Want the figure for your case?

A free half-hour call, no sales deck: we tell you which of the options above you actually need, what it costs and how long it takes to go live.
