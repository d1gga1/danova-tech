What ISH is and what it asks of a stand

ISH is the world's leading trade fair for bathrooms, heating, air conditioning and water. Every two years, in March, Messe Frankfurt brings together manufacturers of sanitary ware, taps, boilers, heat pumps and ventilation with installers, planners and distributors.

Asian manufacturers, especially Chinese producers of heat pumps, valves and components, have a growing presence at ISH alongside European brands. Bathroom products need to be seen installed, as at home; technical products need walls that show systems and connections clearly.

What a stand for ISH needs

  • Full bathroom settings. Tiled or clad walls, installed sanitary ware, mirrors and lighting.
  • Technical walls. Load-bearing structures for boilers, heat pumps and manifolds.
  • Water and drains. Plumbing connections where the regulations allow, for working taps and showers.
  • Explaining efficiency. Screens and graphics on consumption, output and certifications.
  • Meeting area. Tables for installers and distributors.

Exhibiting from Asia or from abroad

Most of our international clients have never worked with a European stand builder before. This is how we make it simple.

  • One contact person. From the first sketch to dismantling you talk to the same person, by email, phone or WhatsApp: in English, or in your language (Chinese, Hindi, Urdu and more) with the help of translation tools.
  • Coordinated crews, warehouse and production. Partner installation crews, our warehouse and trusted workshops for all wood-based structures and furniture, coordinated by one team: one point of control over quality and deadlines.
  • Large and two-storey stands. From small shell scheme stands to custom stands of over 300 m², including two-storey stands with meeting rooms upstairs.
  • We work with your design. If your designer at home has already drawn the stand, we build it, check it against the Italian technical regulations and propose changes only where needed.
  • Contract with your company. We sign and invoice directly with your company, in Asia or anywhere else, with an itemised quote in euro.
  • Approvals and certificates. We submit drawings to the organiser and provide the electrical declaration of conformity and fire-rating certificates.
  • Your freight forwarder, our schedule. Your exhibits travel with your forwarder; we coordinate unloading slots and times so goods and stand arrive in the right order.
  • Storage for the next edition. If you exhibit every year, we keep your stand in our warehouse in Italy and bring it back for the next show.

Yes. We size structures and fixings on the real weight of the units.
