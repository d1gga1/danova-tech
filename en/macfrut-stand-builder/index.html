What Macfrut is and what it asks of a stand

Macfrut is the international exhibition of the fruit and vegetable supply chain, held every spring at Rimini Expo Centre. Growers, cooperatives, exporters, packaging and post-harvest machinery makers, logistics companies and seed and nursery firms meet buyers from retail and wholesale.

Exporters and machinery makers from many countries, China included, exhibit at Macfrut to reach European and Mediterranean buyers. Fresh produce must look fresh for three days under hall lights, and packaging machines need power and space to run.

What a stand for Macfrut needs

  • Fresh produce displays. Crates, shelves and refrigerated counters that keep produce looking good for the whole show.
  • Tasting counters. Preparation area, washing and tasting counter with the right electrical supply.
  • Machinery areas. Platforms and power for sorting, packing and post-harvest machines.
  • Natural materials. Wood, plants and colours that tell the story of the product.
  • Meeting tables. Space to meet buyers and discuss seasons, volumes and logistics.

Exhibiting from China or from abroad

Most of our international clients have never worked with an Italian stand builder before. This is how we make it simple.

  • One contact person. From the first sketch to dismantling you talk to the same person, in English or Chinese, by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • We work with your design. If your designer in China has already drawn the stand, we build it, check it against the Italian technical regulations and propose changes only where needed.
  • Contract with your company. We sign and invoice directly with your company, in China or abroad, with an itemised quote in euro.
  • Approvals and certificates. We submit drawings to the organiser and provide the electrical declaration of conformity and fire-rating certificates.
  • Your freight forwarder, our schedule. Your exhibits travel with your forwarder; we coordinate unloading slots and times so goods and stand arrive in the right order.
  • Storage for the next edition. If you exhibit every year, we keep your stand in our warehouse in Italy and bring it back for the next show.

FAQ

Questions we are often asked.

Can you provide refrigerated displays for fresh produce?

Yes, refrigerated counters and display units on rental, with the electrical supply calculated and ordered from the organiser.

Can we run packaging machines on the stand?

Yes: we plan platforms, power and guards and include them in the project submitted to the organiser.

How much does a stand for Macfrut cost?

It depends on floor space, type of structure, finishes, electrics and services. We do not publish standard prices: we send you a free itemised quote for your project.

Can we communicate in Chinese?

Yes, write to us in Chinese or English. We reply to every request.

Is the stand rented or bought?

As you prefer: shell scheme, modular, fabric or custom-built, rented or bought. If it is yours, we store it for the next edition.
